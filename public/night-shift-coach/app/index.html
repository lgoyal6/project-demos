Which days are you working night shift?
When working less than 7 consecutive night shifts in a row, it is usually better for your body to maintain your daytime circadian rhythm. In this case, you do not need to phase shift your melatonin curve to nights.
When working 7 or more consecutive night shifts, it might be better for your body to phase shift, or "invert", your circadian rhythm to nights. Would you like to do this, or remain on the day rhythm?
